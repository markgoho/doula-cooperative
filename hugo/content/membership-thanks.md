## Welcome to the Rochester Doula Cooperative!

Your membership payment has been processed successfully.

### What Happens Next?

**Check your email** for important next steps:

1. You'll receive a welcome email from us shortly
2. The email contains a link to set up your password
3. Once you set your password, you can access the member portal at [members.doulacooperative.com](https://members.doulacooperative.com)

### Didn't Receive the Email?

If you don't see the welcome email within 10 minutes:

1. **Check your spam folder** - sometimes automated emails end up there
2. **Request a password reset** - Visit [members.doulacooperative.com](https://members.doulacooperative.com) and click "Forgot Password"
3. **Contact us** - Email [webmaster@doulacooperative.com](mailto:webmaster@doulacooperative.com) if you need assistance

### Member Benefits

As a member, you now have access to:

- Informal mentorship and networking opportunities
- Supportive community (in person and online)
- Educational opportunities and professional development
- Option to feature your profile on our website

We're excited to have you as part of our community!
