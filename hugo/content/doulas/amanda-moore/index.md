Hello! I have worked with families in different capacities for over 25 years and have two adult children of my own. I am both birth (2018) and postpartum (2019) doula trained through DONA International and have completed several additional trainings related to birth and the postpartum period. Among them I have also been trained to provide placenta encapsulation. I have a passion to support families at every stage and I am honored to have supported several families in the Rochester area thus far. I am passionate about empowering and advocating for families during their pregnancy, birth, and postpartum journeys.
