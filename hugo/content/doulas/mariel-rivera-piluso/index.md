Hi! I am Mariel, a full-spectrum community-based doula trained since 2018. I offer care for abortions, perinatal loss, birth, postpartum, and a variety of childbirth classes. All of my doula services have in-person and virtual options. Additionally, I am a feminist ethnographer currently completing my PhD research focused on the importance of doulas. I aim to uplift doulas and our clients to help foster safe, happy, and healthy reproductive health experiences. Feel free to reach out for a chat!
