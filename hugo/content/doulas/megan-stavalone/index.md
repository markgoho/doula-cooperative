Dr. Megan Stavalone is a perinatal certified chiropractor who became inspired to become a birth doula after a long and difficult birth with her first child. She is happy to be a part of the team at ANH Wellness, and has extensive expertise in caring for women during the prenatal and postpartum phases. She began attending births in 2023 and is passionate about helping her clients feel empowered and educated during high-risk pregnancy situations or during multi-day inductions, having gone through multiple herself. She is currently accepting new birth doula clients on a limited basis.
