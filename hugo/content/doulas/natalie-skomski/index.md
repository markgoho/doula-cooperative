Hi, I'm Natalie!

I'm a certified birth doula and mom of three, and I know firsthand how overwhelming, beautiful, and deeply personal birth can be. After navigating some challenging births of my own, I felt called to walk alongside other women—helping them feel informed, empowered, and supported every step of the way.

My journey into birth work started over a decade ago when I had the honor of being by a friend's side through labor. Since then, I've had my own babies, learned a lot, and completed my certification through Childbirth International. Now, I get to support women like you through one of life's biggest transitions—and that's a total honor.

Here's some of what's included when we work together:

- Two in-person prenatal consultation
- Unlimited support via text, phone, or email
- Continuous labor and birth support
- One postpartum visits

When I'm not on-call, I'm usually soaking up time with my kiddos, adding to my slightly out-of-control plant collection, or getting messy with a DIY project.

Want to learn more or get a better feel for my approach? Head over to justaskthedoula.com.

And if you have any questions or just want to chat, don't hesitate to reach out, I'd love to hear from you!
