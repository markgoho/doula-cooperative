I'm a licensed acupuncturist, NCCAOM diplomate of acupuncture, doula, and childbirth educator. I have been a birth doula since 2021 and have been doing fertility, pregnancy and postpartum acupuncture since 2022. I am credentialed for in-hopsital acupuncture at both Strong Memorial Hospital and Highland Hospitals.

I'm passionate about women's health and I'm a strong advocate for proper informed consent. I believe that being a doula means supporting the patient in meeting their personal health goals via whatever path they have chosen to take. With that being said, while I do have a holistic mindset, I support my clients and my patients in making the choices that feel right for them and their families. I believe my role is to educate, support and advocate when necessary.

I am primarily focused on acupuncture at this time. Please reach out if you are interested in discussing availability and services.
