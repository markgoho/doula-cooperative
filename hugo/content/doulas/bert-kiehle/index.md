Bert has had birthwork in her heart since she was a little girl. Once she learned what a doula was, she knew this was her ultimate calling! After two years supporting families as a side business, she left her corporate job in 2023 and moved into full-time birthwork! She loves supporting families through pregnancy, birth, & postpartum and is honored to be invited in their journey. Bert is a DONA Certified Birth Doula, DONA Trained Postpartum Doula, Certified Lactation Counselor, Certified VBAC Doula, and Certified Body Ready Method Pro. She is currently accepting clients for all services, please check out her website for more information!
