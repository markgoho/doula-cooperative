Every woman deserves to feel empowered, educated, and supported. These elements are crucial in order to achieve a healthy and positive birthing experience. She is committed to supporting birthing people and families every step of the way. Shannon is dedicated to furthering her training and education to best support each family.

Shannon enjoys living close to and hanging out with her extended family. She enjoys going to the beach, concerts on the shore, swimming, Seabreeze, hiking, and bike riding.
