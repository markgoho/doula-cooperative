# PBI-012: About Doulas Informational Page

## Overview

This PBI covers the creation of an informational page titled "About Doulas" that educates visitors about the different types of doulas, their roles, how to hire a doula, what to expect regarding fees, and answers to common questions. The page will closely follow the layout and content of the Figma frame ([View Figma](https://www.figma.com/design/N1hDApgRTKrIRyu3RdFrFM/Doula-Cooperative?node-id=154-674)).

## Problem Statement

Visitors need a comprehensive, accessible resource to understand what doulas do, the types of support available, how to hire a doula, and what to expect in terms of costs and process. This page aims to demystify doula services and empower users to make informed decisions.

## User Stories

- As a visitor, I want to learn about the different types of doulas, what they do, how to hire one, and what to expect regarding fees, so I can make informed decisions about doula support.

## Technical Approach

- Implement a new page that matches the structure, content, and visual design of the referenced Figma frame.
- Ensure all sections (Birth Doulas, Postpartum Doulas, Other Types of Doulas, Hiring a Doula, Doula Fees, FAQ) are present and accurate.
- Use semantic HTML and accessible markup.
- Ensure the page is responsive and works on all device sizes.
- All text content should be easy to read and well-organized.

## UX/UI Considerations

- Match the Figma design for layout, typography, and color.
- Ensure all sections are visually distinct and easy to navigate.
- FAQ section should be easy to browse and interact with.
- Content should be scannable and not overwhelming.

## Acceptance Criteria

- The About Doulas page matches the layout and content of the Figma frame ([View Figma](https://www.figma.com/design/N1hDApgRTKrIRyu3RdFrFM/Doula-Cooperative?node-id=154-674)).
- Sections for Birth Doulas, Postpartum Doulas, Other Types of Doulas, Hiring a Doula, and Doula Fees are present and match Figma.
- FAQ section includes all questions and answers from Figma.
- All text, buttons, and sections from Figma are present.
- Page is responsive and accessible.
- All content is easy to read and well-organized.
